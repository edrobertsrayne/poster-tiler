Big picture, small printer

Your picture gets cut across ordinary sheets that you tape back together. Nothing is uploaded — it all happens on this page.

01 Choose your picture

A flat PNG or JPEG works best. Layered files need exporting first.

02 Pick paper and direction

Leave direction on Auto unless you want the sheets specifically tall or wide.

03 Choose how big it ends up

Type a finished width and the height follows, so your art is never stretched. Or set DPI instead — higher DPI means a smaller, sharper poster.

04 Check the layout, then print

Red lines show where the sheets meet. Each sheet is labelled — tape r0c0 top-left, r0c1 next to it.
